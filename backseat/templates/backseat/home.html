Every Journey Can Make a Difference.

Backseat never charges a fare. Riders simply share a seat they already have, and travellers who feel moved to give can turn that ride into an act of kindness.

01
Ride

Offer or find a ride

Already travelling alone? Offer your spare seat. Headed the same way? Find someone offering theirs.

02
Go

Travel together, freely

No booking fee, no fare negotiation. You simply share the journey you were already making.

03
QR

Scan the charity QR

At the end of the ride, the rider shows their personal charity QR code, never a payment demand.

04
Give

Donate, if you wish

Choose any amount you like, or none at all. It goes straight to registered charity partners.

This is never a fare. Riders and drivers cannot set, request, or receive a required amount. Every donation is voluntary.

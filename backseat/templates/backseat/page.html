Our Mission

Backseat was created to solve a simple, pervasive reality: millions of empty vehicle seats travel the exact same routes every single day while public transit is crowded and cities face congestion.

Instead of turning commuting into a commercial transaction or fare-setting enterprise, Backseat re-imagines seat-sharing as a community act of generosity. Riders share spare seats for free, and passengers who wish to express gratitude can contribute directly to verified charitable causes.

Crafted by VitalitySoft

VitalitySoft develops purposeful software solutions that connect communities and amplify positive social impact across India.

Core Principles

  • Zero Fares: No rider or driver can ever charge, negotiate, or receive fares.
  • Direct to Charity: 100% of voluntary passenger donations route directly to verified charity partners.
  • Identity Verification: Every rider undergoes vehicle and identity checks before sharing their journey.
